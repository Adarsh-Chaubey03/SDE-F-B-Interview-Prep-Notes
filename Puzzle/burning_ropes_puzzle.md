# Burning Ropes Puzzle

## Question

You have **2 ropes**. Each takes exactly **60 minutes** to burn
completely, but they burn **unevenly**.

How can you measure exactly **45 minutes**?

## Hint

Burning a rope from **both ends** makes its remaining burn-time finish
in half the time.

## Full Solution

1.  At **time 0**, light **both ends of Rope A** and **one end of Rope
    B**.
2.  Rope A finishes after exactly **30 minutes**.
3.  At that moment, light the **other end of Rope B**.
4.  Rope B has **30 minutes of burn-time remaining**.
5.  Burning both ends makes it finish in **15 minutes**.

Therefore:

**30 + 15 = 45 minutes.**

### Key Insight

The ropes burn unevenly, so physical length does not matter. Track
**burn-time**, not length.
