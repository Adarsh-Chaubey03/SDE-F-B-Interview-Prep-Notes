# Water Jug Puzzle

## Question

You have a **5-liter jug** and a **3-liter jug**, with no markings.

Using unlimited water, measure exactly **4 liters**.

## Hint

Use the difference between the two jug capacities to create a controlled
amount.

## Full Solution

1.  Fill the **5 L** jug.
2.  Pour it into the **3 L** jug → **2 L remains** in the 5 L jug.
3.  Empty the **3 L** jug.
4.  Pour the **2 L** into the 3 L jug.
5.  Fill the **5 L** jug again.
6.  Pour from 5 L into the 3 L jug. Since it already has 2 L, only **1
    L** is needed.
7.  Therefore, **4 L remains** in the 5 L jug.

### Answer

**Exactly 4 liters remain in the 5-liter jug.**
